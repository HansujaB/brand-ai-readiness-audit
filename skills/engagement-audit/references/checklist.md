# Engagement review checklist

- Purpose: explicit page type and path; no hostname inference.
- Questions: budget, suitability, returns, plans, service enquiry, documentation and orientation as applicable.
- Evidence: coherent rendered sections; question-specific destinations; product context; no HTTP-only answer pass.
- Browser: stable sample, mobile/touch, scroll coverage, enabled/visible controls, names, blockers, bounds, open shadow roots.
- Reliability: suppress visual decisions when resources fail, the sample is unstable, or embedded frame content is uninspected.
- Read-only: local disclosure/keyboard observations only; only explicit anonymous GET search; no other form/transaction submission.
- Output: concise owner report and separate diagnostics; deduplicated findings, severity counts and explicit limitations.
- Suggestions: observed small targets with spacing/exception review; no generic percentage or superlative advice.
- Performance: instrumented timing and observed session-window CLS; no field or INP claim.
