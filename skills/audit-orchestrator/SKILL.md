For use by an AI agent only. You are the marketplace's designated entrypoint: follow these instructions, compose the specialist checks, review their evidence and emit the final report. Bundled Python scripts are supporting tools; running a script alone does not complete this skill's audit or perform your required review.

Read the three specialist SKILL.md files for their decision procedures and [the report contract](references/schema.md). Compose their checks once through the shared runner.

1. Start one five-minute deadline for collection, review and final validation. Establish one country/language from the request, URL or published website metadata; never silently assume India. Follow only the supplied URL's exact origin (scheme, hostname and effective port), including assets, robots.txt and redirects. Do not fetch news or outside publishers.
2. Run `python skills/audit-orchestrator/scripts/run_audit.py URL --budget 210` from the marketplace root, adding country/language options when supplied. It writes one `<company>_report.json`; use `--brand` for a supplied company name. The workflow samples up to twelve initial pages, three information follow-ups and three representative browser pages. Discovery prioritizes different purposes, then several distinct products or services before pagination. Use `--pages` for relevant same-locale pages when needed; keep collection within the shared deadline.
3. Review both halves of the brief before finalizing: search access and internal discovery; titles and descriptions; applicable structured data and required fields; raw/rendered facts and media alternatives; business identity and fact context; freshness and same-site consistency; visitor answers and public next steps. Use the crawl specialist's checklist to track this internally. A valid JSON block or one sampled product does not establish catalogue-wide readiness. Do not let policy review replace this discoverability pass.
4. Resolve high-impact uncertainty from collected evidence first. Product schema is checked by page purpose and entity, not by the presence of any schema. Distinguish missing required fields from optional enhancements. Match prices to the active offer and compare policy durations for the same event, product and conditions. Inspect clear non-English prose through source-grounded review when automatic patterns do not apply. Targeted same-origin follow-ups must leave time for validation; do not restart a broad crawl or exceed five minutes. Keep source spans, measurements and review questions in memory; write no diagnostics, screenshots, evidence dumps or fact-manifest files.
5. Write a short action list for a business owner: one title, severity, evidence sentence, two concise fix sentences and one verification sentence per distinct problem. Merge repeated fixes across pages. Select up to eight priority findings and up to three improvements; include no more than three example page links per finding. Prefer higher severity and wider impact, considering discoverability and engagement when priorities tie. Count only listed items in `summary` and `improvements_summary`; if additional findings were omitted, disclose their number in the brief note. Keep important assessment limitations in that same note. Do not add coverage objects, scores, request counts or raw technical data. A short report must come from broad assessment and consolidation, not skipped checks. No invented findings or ranking guarantees.
6. Validate and recount both summaries using the filename printed by the runner, for example `python skills/audit-orchestrator/scripts/validate_report.py lifestyle_report.json --write`. Update that same file after review and deliver only it. Source collection, evidence review and validation share the original deadline; an incomplete run gets a brief note, not extra report files.

All collection remains anonymous, read-only and robots-respecting. No authenticated pages, forms that change state, cart/checkout operations or private destinations. Tool failures are assessment limitations, never website defects. Keep detailed technical measurements inside the audit; the owner's action and verification should explain what to change and how to check the outcome in plain language.
