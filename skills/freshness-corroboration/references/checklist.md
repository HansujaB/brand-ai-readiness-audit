# Website claim review

- Extract claims from this website only. Compare one country/language at a time.
- Bind product prices to product identity, variant, currency, active date and conditions. Unresolved scope is a review item, not an automatic conflict.
- For delivery, returns and warranty, quote the full sentence and inspect nearby exceptions. Preserve ranges and business-day units. Different service areas, product categories or historical periods can explain different durations.
- Compare explicitly labelled update dates to equivalent structured dates on the same article. Missing dates and publication age alone do not demonstrate stale facts.
- Read the target fact and its applicable conditions independently before testing isolated extraction. A single-offer conditions section may qualify that offer; another product's section or an unrelated footer does not.
- Automatic duration matching currently recognizes English and a limited Hindi/French/German/Spanish vocabulary. It does not establish semantic completeness in any language. Resolve unrecognized claim types and translations by reading actual source content; preserve quotes in the selected language.
- Each unresolved item needs its competing spans, the uncertain dimension, and a same-site evidence step. Each confirmed issue needs a specific correction and verification. Opportunity priority depends on impact, not whether it is classified as a defect.
